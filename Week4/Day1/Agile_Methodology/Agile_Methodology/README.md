# 🚀 Intro to Agile Development (Scrum)

In this session, you’ll learn **how** Agile works and **why** it’s one of the most powerful methodologies used by modern software development teams around the world.

---

## 💡 Why Agile?

Traditional development models (like Waterfall) are:
- Rigid
- Linear
- Slow to adapt to feedback or changes

Agile solves these problems by being:
- Flexible
- Iterative
- Focused on delivering **working software quickly**

Agile is used because it:
- Embraces change at any stage
- Improves collaboration between team members
- Increases visibility and accountability
- Leads to higher product quality and user satisfaction

---

## ⚙️ How Agile Works – Scrum in Action

### 1. **Roles**
- 🧑‍💼 **Product Owner** – sets the vision and priorities
- 🧑‍🏫 **Scrum Master** – coaches the team, removes obstacles
- 👨‍💻 **Development Team** – builds the product

### 2. **Ceremonies (Events)**
- 🗓️ **Sprint Planning** – plan what to build this sprint
- 🌅 **Daily Standup** – team sync every day
- 🧪 **Sprint Review** – demo the result
- 🪞 **Sprint Retrospective** – reflect and improve

### 3. **Artifacts**
- 📌 **Product Backlog** – to-do list for the product
- 🧩 **Sprint Backlog** – what the team commits to for this sprint
- 🧱 **Increment** – the completed, working software

---

## 🛠️ Real-World Example

Imagine you're building a **To-Do App**:
- Instead of building everything in one go, you break it down into smaller parts (user stories).
- You plan and deliver a usable feature every 1–2 weeks (called a Sprint).
- You get feedback early, adjust priorities, and improve continuously.

This is exactly how Agile helps real teams succeed — and how we’ll work in your upcoming bootcamp projects too!
